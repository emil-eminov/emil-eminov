# Hi, I'm Emil Eminov

**PhD candidate in Cyber Studies at The University of Tulsa (Cyber Fellow).** I research fraud and anomaly detection in digital platform economies: live-streaming monetization, in-game asset markets, and the adversaries who adapt to beat detection. Before the PhD I audited identity and access controls at PwC and built security tooling for the automotive industry at ESCRYPT (Bosch) and BMW.

[Website](https://emileminov.com) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emil-eminov) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=R4GXi28AAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-0362-2397) · emil.eminov.95@gmail.com

Based in Tulsa, Oklahoma and open to relocating. I work in English, German, Turkish and Azerbaijani.
